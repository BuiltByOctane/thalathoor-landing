# Third-party brand marks

These are the trademarks of their respective owners, not of Thalathoor Heritage
Homestay. They are used here solely to identify and link to this business's own
profile on each platform — nominative use, which is what both brands publish
these assets for.

| File | Mark | Source |
|---|---|---|
| `instagram.svg` | Instagram glyph | Official 2016 glyph, via Wikimedia Commons |
| `tripadvisor.svg` | Tripadvisor circle owl | Icon portion of the official horizontal lockup (green circle `#34E0A1` + owl), via Wikimedia Commons |

Both were minified with svgo and rendered to confirm they match the originals.

Rules to keep to if these are ever edited:

- Do not recolour, rotate, stretch or add effects to either mark.
- Keep the aspect ratio square and leave clear space around them.
- `tripadvisor.svg` keeps the official green; do not substitute the site palette.
- If a mark needs replacing, take it from the brand's own resource page rather
  than redrawing it. An approximated trademark is a misused trademark.
